01 / Start a stage

Every match opens with one stage, ready to go

The calculator loads with a single card named Stage 1. Nothing to configure first — just start entering what's in front of you: the shot timer's raw time, then your hits.

Everything you type is saved to this browser automatically, so closing the tab or reloading mid-match won't lose your stages.

04 / Log time and hits

Type the raw time, then tap through your hits

Enter the shot timer's raw time in the Time field. Then use the − / + steppers on each row — A, C, D, Miss, No-Shoot (NS), and Procedural (Proc) — to build up the hit count. Every tap recalculates instantly.

05 / Read the score

Points and Hit Factor update on every tap

Points, at the bottom of the card, is your zone hits minus penalties: (16×5) + (2×4) − (1×0) = 88.

Hit Factor, up on the screen, is points divided by time: 88 ÷ 14.32 = 6.2151, shown to four decimal places — the same precision as a printed score sheet.

No-shoots and procedurals are flat −10 penalties regardless of power factor, and they can push points (and hit factor) negative if they outweigh your hits.

07 / Build out the match

Add and remove stages as you go

+ Add Stage, at the bottom of the list, appends a fresh blank card. Each card's × removes just that stage — it's hidden on the last remaining one, so you always have at least one stage to work with.

Hit factors aren't summed across stages here, since USPSA match scoring is percentage-based, not additive — this tool scores one stage at a time, the way you'd fill in one line of a score sheet.
